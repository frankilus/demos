Panel upgrade: check capacity first.

If breakers are tripping, the first question on the call is capacity — what the panel can handle today, and what the new load needs. Mention every large new load (EV charger, remodel, added circuits) so the visit is planned around it.

  • Ask whether a capacity check is the first step.
  • Note which breakers trip, and when.
  • Have the panel's age and any past work handy for the call.

Questions customers actually ask

Do I need a main panel upgrade?

If breakers trip often, the panel is old, or you are adding a large load such as an EV charger or a remodel, a capacity check is the honest first step. The upgrade itself — and its price — is confirmed after that check, not guessed from this page.

What does rewiring an older home involve?

House rewires replace aging wiring, planned room by room so the home stays livable during the work. Older homes (especially those built before 1970) are the most likely candidates — tell the office the home's age and any known wiring type on the first call.

Can I get an EV charger at home?

Yes — EV charger installation is a listed service. The panel's capacity is checked first, because the charger is a large new load. Mention the charger type and where the car parks when you call.

The family story, 1972 – today

SoHo has reinvented itself a dozen times since 1972. Through every era — artists' lofts, cast-iron restorations, rooftop terraces — one thing hasn't changed: the name on the truck, and the family standing behind the work.

The first generation

A ladder, a truck, and a SoHo workshop

In 1972 the family sets up shop in SoHo, taking on the roofs nobody else wants — the old cast-iron buildings with their awkward pitches, aging tar, and skylights older than the crew. Word of mouth does the rest.

The second generation

The kids who swept the shop take the roof

The second generation grows up in the business — weekends on tear-offs, summers learning flashing by hand. As SoHo's lofts convert and rooftops become terraces and gardens, Manhattan Roofing grows with the neighborhood it started in.

The third generation

Same standards, sharper tools

Today the third generation runs the crews — drone inspections and photo reports alongside the same rule the founder started with: walk every roof yourself before you quote it, and never sell a homeowner a roof they don't need.

What we do up there

Every job starts the same way: a free on-site estimate. We come out, walk the roof, show you photos of what we find, and give you a written quote — no obligation, no pressure.

A.

Leak repair

Active leak? We trace the water to its actual entry point — flashing, seams, skylights — and fix the cause, not just the stain on your ceiling.

B.

Flat & low-slope roofs

The classic New York roof. Coatings, patches, and full replacements for the flat and low-slope roofs over lofts, brownstones, and commercial buildings.

C.

Shingle roofs

Tear-offs and re-roofs with proper underlayment and ventilation, matched to the character of the building — and cleaned up like we were never there.

D.

Skylights & flashing

Skylight replacement and resealing, chimney and parapet flashing — the detail work where most New York leaks are actually born.

E.

Gutters & drainage

Gutters, leaders, and roof drains sized for a New York downpour, so water leaves the roof the way it's supposed to.

F.

Roof inspections

Buying, selling, or just overdue? A documented inspection with photos, so you know the roof's real condition before the weather tells you.

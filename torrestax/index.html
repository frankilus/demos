Before You Come In

What to bring

Ten minutes of gathering now saves a second trip later. For a personal return, bring:

  • Photo ID — and Social Security or ITIN cards for everyone on the return
  • W-2s and 1099s — from every job and payer this year
  • Last year's tax return — if you have it
  • Income & expense records — self-employment, rental, or side work
  • Bank details — routing and account numbers for direct-deposit refunds
  • Any IRS or state letters — don't leave them in the envelope

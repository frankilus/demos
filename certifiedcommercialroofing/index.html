06 · COMMON QUESTIONS

Common questions, answered straight.

Flat roof or TPO — which should I choose?

It depends on your building. A flat / low-slope roof is defined by its shape and drainage; TPO is one membrane system that can cover it, with heat-welded seams. The selector above compares the systems, and the final recommendation is confirmed after an inspection of your deck, drainage, and use — not from a web page alone.

What does the 2-year warranty on repairs cover?

Repairs carry a 2-year warranty. The exact scope of a given repair is confirmed by phone before work begins, so there is no ambiguity about what was repaired and what is backed.

How does storm damage repair work?

Report the damage, get an inspection and assessment after severe weather, then a confirmed scope before work begins. Repairs are backed by the 2-year repair warranty.

When is it time for an inspection or replacement?

If a roof is older, has visible damage, or shows leaks after storms, it is time for a professional inspection. An inspection flags age and damage early so owners can plan a repair or replacement instead of reacting to a failure.

Are you licensed and insured? Do you offer financing?

Yes — Certified Commercial Roofing is fully licensed, insured, and certified. Financing is available, with terms confirmed by phone.

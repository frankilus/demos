What Airnizer does

Ductless, done properly.

01

Mini-split design & installation

Load-sized systems for homes with no ducts — or no room for them. Wall, floor, and ceiling units matched to each space.

02

Heating & cooling in one system

Heat-pump mini-splits warm in winter as efficiently as they cool in summer, so one organized system covers the whole year.

03

Multi-zone home plans

One outdoor unit, up to a whole home of independent zones — each room on its own temperature, schedule, and remote.

04

Seasonal maintenance

Spring and fall tune-ups that keep filters, coils, and drains ahead of the weather — book them in the scheduler above.

05

Service & repair

Weak airflow, odd smells, a zone that won't keep up — diagnosis and repair for existing ductless systems.

06

Rental & multi-family units

Per-apartment comfort for owners and managers: independent zones tenants control, without window units in every frame.

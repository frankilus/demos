Questions

        
Before you ask

      

      

        
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
Walk-ins are welcome during office hours. An appointment simply guarantees your time — recommended in March and early April, when the office is busiest.

        
How much does it cost to file?
Fees depend on the forms your return needs — a simple W-2 return is very different from a business return with schedules. Call (718) 847-5708, describe your situation, and we'll give you a straightforward quote before any work begins. No surprises at the desk.

        
How fast will I get my refund?
We e-file, which is the fastest route. The IRS typically issues refunds within 21 days of acceptance for e-filed returns with direct deposit — we'll show you how to track yours.

        
I haven't filed in a few years. Can you help?
Yes — it's more common than you think, and it is almost always fixable. We prepare prior-year returns and get you current. The sooner you start, the more options you have.

        
I got a letter from the IRS. What do I do?
Don't ignore it, and don't assume it's correct. Bring the letter to the office — we'll explain what it says, what (if anything) you owe, and how to respond by the deadline printed on it.

        
Can you help my small business year-round?
Yes. Beyond the annual return, we help with quarterly estimates, sales-tax and payroll questions, and simple record-keeping habits that make next year's filing painless.

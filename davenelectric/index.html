Violations & permits, explained

General information only — not legal or code advice. Your violation paperwork controls the deadlines and requirements.

What is a code / ECB violation correction?

A violation notice says an electrical condition must be fixed to meet code. Correction usually means licensed electrical work plus the paperwork and re-inspection needed to clear it. Bring the violation number and any DOB/ECB paperwork to your first call.

What is permit remediation?

Permit remediation brings unpermitted or stalled electrical work into compliance — permits, filings and inspections so the work is properly documented and signed off.

What does a safety inspection cover?

A safety inspection checks panels, wiring, grounding, lighting and devices for hazards and code issues, and produces a punch list of what should be fixed first — especially useful before a sale, renovation or tenant turnover.

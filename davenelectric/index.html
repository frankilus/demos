Plain-English explainer

Permits & violations, decoded

No jargon wall. The four questions every manager asks first.

What's the difference between a permit and a violation?

A permit is permission obtained before work — it says the job is filed and will be inspected. A violation is issued after a problem is found — work without a permit, unsafe conditions, or failed inspection items. Most violation fixes require permitted, corrected work plus a sign-off.

I got a DOB / ECB notice. What actually happens if I ignore it?

Deadlines, fines, and hearings can stack up, and an open violation can complicate sales, refinancing, and tenant issues. The practical move is the same either way: identify the exact item, correct it with the right filing, and document the clearance.

Do I need an inspection before I call?

No. A safety inspection is often the first paid step — it maps what's live, what's outdated, and what an inspector will flag. That report becomes the scope for correction work, so you're not paying to guess.

What should I have ready for a faster quote?

Building address, building type, a photo of the panel if it's a panel issue, the violation notice if you have one, and access notes (super, tenant, after-hours). The estimator below packages all of that for you.

Good to know before you call

General information only — your job's scope, safety requirements, and pricing are confirmed by phone.

What should I have ready for a panel upgrade call?

Your panel's location, its approximate age, and what is tripping or running short — a photo of the panel label helps the conversation, and the installer confirms the rest on site.

What should I have ready for an EV charger call?

The charger model (or the vehicle), where you park, and how far that spot is from your panel. The installer confirms circuit capacity and routing before any work starts.

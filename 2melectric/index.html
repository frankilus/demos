    Questions, answered straight

    What are the signs I need a panel upgrade?

    Repeated tripping, flickering or dimming lights, a panel that is decades old, or no spare capacity when adding a major load such as an EV charger or air conditioning. Mention the signs on the call and the installer confirms whether an upgrade is needed.

    What should I have ready for an EV charger call?

    The vehicle and charger model if you have them, where you park, and how far that spot is from your panel. The installer confirms circuit capacity and routing before any work starts.

    Is 2M Electric licensed?
